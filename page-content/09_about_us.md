## SECTION 1: OUR STORY

### Heading (H2)
**How DesignXer Started**

DesignXer was founded on a simple observation: Small businesses were getting squeezed from both sides.

On one side, they could use cheap DIY website builders (Wix, Squarespace) that looked generic and didn't drive real results. On the other side, they couldn't afford $50K+ from high-end agencies that didn't understand the unique challenges of small businesses.

**We saw the gap.**

We realized there was a huge opportunity to serve businesses at the $5K-$25K range—companies serious about growth but realistic about budgets. Companies that wanted:
- Custom design (not templates)
- Real SEO expertise (not just fancy words)
- Genuine partnership (not just a hand-off and goodbye)
- Transparent communication (not hidden fees)
- Results that matter (not vanity metrics)

**So we started DesignXer to fill that gap.**

Today, we've helped 200+ businesses transform their online presence, generating millions in attributed revenue for our clients.
