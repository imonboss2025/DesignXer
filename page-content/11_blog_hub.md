## BLOG STRUCTURE RECOMMENDATIONS

**Publishing Frequency:** 4 blog posts/month (1 per week)

**Content Mix:**
- 40% How-to/Tutorial posts
- 30% Industry insights/trends
- 20% Case studies
- 10% Opinion/thought leadership

**Content Calendar:**
- Plan 3 months ahead
- Include seasonal topics
- Tie to industry events
- Respond to client questions

**Promotion Strategy:**
- Email newsletter
- Social media sharing
- Internal linking
- Guest post opportunities
- Podcast mentions
