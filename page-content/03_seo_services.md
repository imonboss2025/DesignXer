## SECTION 6: GOOGLE'S CORE WEB VITALS & SEO

### Heading (H2)
**Why Page Speed Matters for SEO**

Google now uses page speed as a ranking factor through Core Web Vitals. We optimize all three:

### Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) (H3)
How fast your main content loads. Target: Under 2.5 seconds.

### Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) (H3)
How stable your page is during loading. Target: Less than 0.1.

### Interaction to Next Paint (INP) (H3)
How responsive your page is to interactions. Target: Under 200ms.

**Our optimization improves all three, which improves both rankings and user experience.**
