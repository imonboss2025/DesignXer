## Introduction (H1)

Follow proven eCommerce design practices to reduce friction, increase average order value, and turn first-time visitors into repeat buyers.

---

## 1. Product Page Optimization (H2)

- High-quality images with zoom and multiple views
- Short benefit-led headline + concise description
- Prominent price, stock status, and shipping info
- Reviews and social proof near CTA

---

## 2. Simplify Checkout (H2)

- Minimal form fields, guest checkout, and progress indicators
- Autofill, saved payment methods, and clear error messaging
- Transparent shipping and returns info before payment

---

## 3. Search & Navigation (H2)

- Fast, faceted search with autocomplete and filters
- Category pages that match user intent and support SEO

---

## 4. Mobile Commerce (H2)

- Thumb-friendly buttons, one-tap actions, and visible CTAs
- Mobile-optimized product listing and checkout flows

---

## 5. Personalization & Recommendations (H2)

- Related products, recently viewed, and AI-driven recommendations
- Email and onsite personalization to recover carts and suggest cross-sells

---

## 6. Performance & Trust (H2)

- Fast load times, secure checkout, and visible trust badges
- Clear refund and shipping policies

---

## Implementation Checklist (H2)

- Audit product pages for missing elements
- Run checkout usability tests
- Implement faceted search and filters
- Set up RUM for mobile performance
