## Introduction (H1)

Choosing between WordPress and custom development depends on project requirements, budget, timeline, and long-term maintenance preferences. This guide helps you decide.

---

## 1. When WordPress Makes Sense (H2)

- Content-rich sites, blogs, marketing sites, small eCommerce (WooCommerce)
- Faster time-to-market and lower initial cost
- Large plugin ecosystem for common needs

---

## 2. When Custom Development Is Better (H2)

- Unique product requirements, complex integrations, or large-scale performance needs
- Full control over architecture and deployment
- Higher initial cost but tailored scalability

---

## 3. Maintenance & Ownership (H2)

- WordPress: frequent updates, plugin compatibility issues, easier non-dev edits
- Custom: controlled deployments, dependency management, developer-led updates

---

## 4. SEO & Performance Implications (H2)

- Both can be performant; WordPress needs careful optimization and selective plugin use
- Custom builds can optimize only the needed features for performance-critical sites

---

## 5. Cost & Timeline Comparison (H2)

- WordPress: $3K–$20K, 2–8 weeks for typical sites
- Custom: $20K+, 8+ weeks depending on scope and integrations
