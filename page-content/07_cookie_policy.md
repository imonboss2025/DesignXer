## 1. WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone, etc.). They help websites recognize your device, remember your preferences, and track your behavior for analytics and marketing purposes.

**Cookies typically contain:**
- Website name
- Expiration date
- Randomly generated identifier
- Stored information (preferences, login data, etc.)

---

## 2. WHY WE USE COOKIES

We use cookies to:
- **Improve functionality:** Remember your preferences
- **Measure performance:** Track visitor behavior and usage
- **Deliver better experience:** Personalize content
- **Marketing:** Retarget ads, measure campaign effectiveness
- **Security:** Protect against fraud and unauthorized access
- **Analytics:** Understand how you use our website

## 3. TYPES OF COOKIES WE USE

### 3.1 Essential/Necessary Cookies

**Purpose:** Required for basic website functionality

**Examples:**

**Providers:** Google Ads, Facebook Pixel, LinkedIn, etc.  
**Examples:**

- Audience segmentation

**Your Choice:** Opt-out available

### 3.5 Social Media Cookies

**Purpose:** Integration with social media platforms  
**Duration:** Varies

**Examples:**

## 6. HOW TO CONTROL COOKIES

### 6.1 Browser Controls

Most browsers allow you to:
- **View cookies:** See stored cookies
- **Delete cookies:** Remove stored cookies
- **Block cookies:** Prevent new cookies
- **Clear history:** Remove all browsing data

**Browser Instructions:**
- **Chrome:** chrome://settings/privacy
- **Firefox:** preferences/privacy
- **Safari:** preferences/privacy
- **Edge:** settings/privacy
- **Internet Explorer:** tools/internet options

### 6.2 Cookie Consent Settings

When you visit our site, you can:
- **Accept All:** Accept all cookies
- **Customize:** Choose which types
- **Reject Non-Essential:** Reject marketing cookies only
- **Change Later:** Access settings anytime

### 6.3 Opt-Out Services

**Global Opt-Out:**
- Network Advertising Initiative: optout.networkadvertising.org
- Digital Advertising Alliance: optout.aboutads.info

**Google Analytics Opt-Out:**
- Google Analytics Opt-Out Extension
- tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

**Google Ads Opt-Out:**
- myaccount.google.com/advertisingpreferences

**Facebook Opt-Out:**
- facebook.com/settings?tab=ads

## 8. COOKIE DURATION

Cookies are either:

### Session Cookies
- **Duration:** Deleted when you close browser
- **Purpose:** Essential functionality
- **Example:** Login sessions

### Persistent Cookies
- **Duration:** Remain for specified period (days/months/years)
- **Purpose:** Analytics, preferences, marketing
- **Example:** Remembering your language choice

Most of our persistent cookies expire within:
- 13 months (Google Analytics)
- 2 years (Performance cookies)
