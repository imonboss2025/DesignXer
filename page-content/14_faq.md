## GENERAL QUESTIONS (H2)

### What types of businesses do you work with? (H3)
We primarily serve small and medium-sized businesses across the USA and Canada, including eCommerce, professional services, SaaS, health & wellness, and local service providers.

### Do you only build websites or offer marketing services too? (H3)
We offer full-service web design, development, and SEO, plus content strategy, CRO, and ongoing maintenance.

### Are you remote or do you meet in person? (H3)
We are remote-first and serve clients nationwide; in-person meetings are available in select cities by appointment.

### How do we start? (H3)
Start by scheduling a free 30-minute consultation or submitting the contact form with project details.

### Do you sign NDAs? (H3)
Yes. We can sign an NDA or engage under confidentiality terms within our contract.

### Do you work with agencies or take white-label work? (H3)
Yes — we offer partnerships and white-label services for agencies and resellers.

## SERVICES & PROCESS (H2)

### What is your typical project process? (H3)
Our five-phase process: Discovery → Strategy & Planning → Design → Development & Launch → Optimization & Growth.

### How long does a website project take? (H3)
Typical timelines: Simple brochure sites (6–8 weeks), small eCommerce (10–16 weeks), custom platforms (3–6+ months).

### Can you redesign our existing site? (H3)
Yes — we offer redesigns, platform migrations, and performance optimization for existing sites.

### Do you provide content writing and photography? (H3)
Yes — we can provide content creation, copywriting, and partner with photographers for visual assets.

### Will my site be mobile-friendly? (H3)
All sites are built mobile-first and responsive across devices.

### What platforms do you build on? (H3)
Common platforms: WordPress (custom themes), Webflow, Shopify for eCommerce, and custom frameworks when needed.

## PRICING & CONTRACTS (H2)

### How much does a website cost? (H3)
Our packages typically start at $5K for starter sites, $10K–25K for growth-level projects, and $25K+ for enterprise/custom solutions. Exact pricing depends on scope.

### Do you offer payment plans? (H3)
Yes — we offer milestone payments, retainers for ongoing services, and flexible payment options.

### Are deposits refundable? (H3)
Deposits are generally non-refundable once work begins; specific terms are in the contract.

### Do you charge for revisions? (H3)
Revisions within the agreed scope are included. Out-of-scope changes are billed hourly per our terms.

### What is included in your SEO retainers? (H3)
SEO retainers commonly include technical fixes, on-page optimization, content strategy, monthly reporting, and outreach/link work as agreed in the scope.

## TIMELINES & DELIVERABLES (H2)

### When will we see results from SEO? (H3)
SEO is a medium-to-long-term strategy. Expect foundational improvements in 3–6 months and compounding results over 6–12+ months.

### Do you provide timelines and milestones? (H3)
Yes — every proposal includes a detailed timeline, milestones, and acceptance criteria.

### What happens after launch? (H3)
We offer post-launch support, monitoring, and optimization packages. We monitor performance, fix issues, and execute agreed growth strategies.

## TECHNICAL & SECURITY (H2)

### Who owns the website code and content? (H3)
Clients retain ownership of their content. Code ownership details are specified in the contract; typically we license our code or transfer ownership after final payment where agreed.

### Do you provide backups and security? (H3)
Yes — we include baseline security hardening and can provide backup and maintenance plans.

### Will my site be accessible? (H3)
We follow accessibility best practices (WCAG recommendations) and can include an accessibility audit as part of the scope.

### Do you handle hosting? (H3)
We provide hosting recommendations and can manage hosting on behalf of clients (managed WordPress hosting, Cloudflare, CDN setups).
