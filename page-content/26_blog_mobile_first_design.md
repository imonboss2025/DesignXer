## Introduction (H1)

Mobile-first design means designing for the smallest screen first, then scaling up — ensuring the experience is efficient, accessible, and conversion-ready on mobile devices.

---

## 1. Why Mobile-First? (H2)

- Majority of users browse on mobile; search now prioritizes mobile experience
- Mobile constraints force clarity and performance improvements
- Mobile-first improves Core Web Vitals and conversion rates

---

## 2. Key Principles (H2)

- Prioritize content and actions for mobile users
- Thumb-friendly controls and simplified navigation
- Prioritize loading critical content first (lazy-load below-the-fold)

---

## 3. Design Techniques (H2)

- Simplified hero with clear CTA
- Collapsible content and progressive disclosure
- Responsive images and adaptive content blocks

---

## 4. Testing for Mobile (H2)

- Real-device testing across OS and browsers
- Use RUM and synthetic tests to track performance
- Usability testing for forms, menus, and checkout flows

---

## 5. Accessibility & Mobile (H2)

- Larger hit targets, color contrast, readable font sizes
- Ensure gestures and interactions are discoverable and accessible
