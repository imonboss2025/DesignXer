Cookie Policy

Last Updated: May 14, 2026  ·  Effective Date: May 14, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device (computer, tablet, smartphone). They help websites recognize your device, remember your preferences, and track behavior for analytics and marketing purposes.

Cookies typically contain: the website name, an expiration date, a randomly generated identifier, and stored information such as preferences or login data.

2. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to improve functionality (remember preferences), measure performance (track behavior), deliver a better experience (personalize content), support marketing (retarget ads, measure campaigns), enhance security (protect against fraud), and understand analytics (how you use our website).

3. Types of Cookies We Use

3.1 Essential / Necessary Cookies

Required for basic website functionality. Cannot be disabled — required for the site to work. Examples include session authentication, security cookies, and load balancing cookies.

3.2 Performance / Analytics Cookies

Measure website performance and user behavior. Duration: 6–24 months. Providers: Google Analytics. Covers page views, traffic sources, time on page, bounce rates, and device/browser info. Opt-out available.

3.3 Functional Cookies

Remember preferences and settings including theme preference, language selection, display preferences, and accessibility settings. Duration: session to 2 years. Opt-out available.

3.4 Marketing / Advertising Cookies

Track for advertising and remarketing. Duration: 6 months to 2 years. Providers: Google Ads, Facebook Pixel, LinkedIn. Used for retargeting ads, audience segmentation, and conversion tracking. Opt-out available.

3.5 Social Media Cookies

Integration with social media platforms for social sharing tracking and embedded social content. Duration varies. Depends on social platform settings.

6. How to Control Cookies

6.1 Browser Controls

Most browsers allow you to view, delete, or block cookies. Instructions:

  • Chrome: chrome://settings/privacy
  • Firefox: Preferences › Privacy & Security
  • Safari: Preferences › Privacy
  • Edge: Settings › Privacy, search, and services

6.2 Opt-Out Services

Global Opt-Out: Network Advertising Initiative — optout.networkadvertising.org

Google Analytics Opt-Out: tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

Google Ads Opt-Out: myaccount.google.com/advertisingpreferences

Facebook Opt-Out: facebook.com/settings?tab=ads

7. Do Not Track (DNT)

We recognize and honor Do Not Track (DNT) browser signals. Users with DNT enabled will not be targeted with tracking. Note: DNT support varies by browser and third-party service.

8. Cookie Duration

Session Cookies: Deleted when you close your browser. Used for essential functionality like login sessions.

Persistent Cookies: Remain for a specified period. Most of our persistent cookies expire within 13 months (Google Analytics), 2 years (performance cookies), or 2 years (advertising cookies).

9. Cookies & Privacy

Cookies can contain personal information. See our Privacy Policy for full details about what information we collect, how we use it, your privacy rights, and how we protect your data.

10. International Compliance

GDPR (EU/EEA)

We obtain consent before non-essential cookies, provide clear opt-out options, allow withdrawal of consent at any time, and periodically re-prompt for consent.

CCPA (California)

We disclose cookies and tracking, provide opt-out rights, apply cookie use restrictions, and do not discriminate for opt-out.

PIPEDA (Canada)

We notify about cookies at first visit, allow cookie preferences, honor browser preferences, and renew consent regularly.

11. Impact of Disabling Cookies

Basic website functionality and page viewing will still work. However, disabling non-essential cookies means preferences won't be remembered, personalized content won't be shown, conversion tracking won't function, and login sessions may not persist. Essential cookies cannot be disabled — they are required for the site to function.

12. Cookie Security

We protect cookies through HTTPS encryption, the Secure flag (transmitted over HTTPS only), HttpOnly flag (not accessible to JavaScript), SameSite attribute (CSRF protection), limited storage (only necessary data), and regular cleanup of old cookies.

13. Changes to Cookie Policy

We may update this policy if we add new cookies, if regulations change (GDPR, CCPA), or to improve clarity. We'll post updates and may prompt for new consent if required. Continued use of the Website constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
